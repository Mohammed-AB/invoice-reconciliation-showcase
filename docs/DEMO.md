# Invoice Reconciliation: guided demonstration

English/Arabic synthetic document packets. Fixture extraction and real text-layer parsing are distinct modes; image-only OCR remains unverified.

## A focused walkthrough

1. **Open a packet.**
2. **compare lines.**
3. **inspect an exception.**
4. **approve or resolve.**

Pause on the evidence at each decision: arabic / english extraction; document-version tracking; review and approval boundaries.
